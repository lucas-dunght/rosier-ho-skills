# Short-Form Viral Video 7-Dimension Analysis Framework

> Distilled from real-world TikTok Shop, Reels, and Douyin e-commerce creative testing.
> Use this framework to evaluate competitor videos and structure new Vietnamese scripts.

## The 7 Analysis Dimensions

### 1. Hook Analysis (First 3 Seconds) ⭐ Most Critical Dimension

> The first 3 seconds determine survival. **Hook = Copy Hook + Visual Hook**-both tracks must fire simultaneously.
> Great copy with a dull visual gets scrolled past; explosive visuals with weak copy fail to qualify the buyer.

**6 Copy Hook Archetypes (With Natural Vietnamese Examples)**:

| Archetype | Definition | Vietnamese Example | Best For |
|-----------|------------|--------------------|----------|
| **Counter-Intuitive** | Shatter common beliefs, create cognitive conflict | *"90% chị em đang rửa mặt sai cách mỗi ngày mà không hề biết!"* | Education, product testing, skincare |
| **Pain Point** | Hit an acute, frustrating problem head-on | *"Thâm mụn cả năm không mờ nổi dù đã bôi đủ thứ?"* | Skincare, health, personal care |
| **Data Anchor** | Use specific, striking numbers to command attention | *"1 viên sủi này chứa 250mg Vitamin C · bằng ăn 6 quả cam tươi!"* | Functional products, supplements, tech |
| **Relatable Scenario** | Recreate an everyday moment the viewer immediately recognizes | *"Cứ trước hôm nào đi hẹn hò là mình phải đắp ngay miếng này..."* | Daily essentials, beauty, fashion |
| **Curiosity Gap** | Withhold a key detail to trigger intense curiosity | *"Đừng để chồng bạn biết giá thật của bộ nồi này nhé..."* | Household, gadgets, lifestyle |
| **Direct Benefit / Offer** | Lead with an irresistible deal or value stack | *"Mua 1 tặng 6, tính ra chưa tới 15 cành một tuýp!"* | Flash sales, mega campaigns, bundles |

**6 Visual Hook Archetypes (Must Synchronize with Copy Hook)**:

| Archetype | Technique | Example | Retention Principle |
|-----------|-----------|---------|---------------------|
| **Visual Spectacle** | CGI micro-world, stop-motion, unusual macro shot | Miniature animation cleaning plaque between teeth | "Never seen this before" → stops thumb immediately |
| **Product Stacking** | Massive bundle piling into frame, spinning tray | "Buy 1 Get 8" items dropping rapidly onto the table | "So much stuff!" → triggers bargain hunter instinct |
| **Abrupt Visual Contrast** | Split-screen Before/After, instant color/state flip | Stained yellow surface → sparkling clean in 1 second | Stark visual contrast grabs the eye |
| **High-Impact Action** | Exaggerated reaction, fast hand motion, tearing/crushing | Squeezing a thick ribbon of cream or ripping open a box | Motion beats static frames |
| **Pop-Up Kinetic Text** | Giant bold headline, flying price tag, red "X" overlay | Giant red "❌ SAI LẦM!" stamped across the screen | Instant message delivery in 0.5 seconds |
| **First-Person POV** | Handheld showcase, overhead desk shot, direct eye contact | Holding the item right up to the lens like showing a friend | High immersion and personal trust |

**Mandatory Checkpoints When Analyzing a Competitor Video**:
- **Copy Hook**: Archetype, verbatim transcript (translated to Vietnamese if foreign), and relevance to the product
- **Visual Hook**: Archetype and concrete description (shot size, color grading, camera movement, VFX)
- **Copy × Visual Synergy**: Do the voiceover and visual action reinforce the exact same point at the exact same second?
- **Frame 0.0s Test**: If the video is paused at 0.0s, does that single frame compel a click/watch?
- **Sound Design**: Are there whooshes, dings, BGM drops, or vocal tone shifts in the first 3 seconds?
- **Hook Strength Score (1–10)** with concrete justification

### 2. Storyboard Structure

**Shot-by-Shot Breakdown**:
- Duration and shot size of every cut (Extreme Close-Up / Close-Up / Medium Shot / Wide Shot / Overhead POV)
- Transition style between cuts (Hard cut / Jump cut / Whip pan / Dissolve)
- Ratio of total cuts to video length (cut density / pacing speed)

**Standard Structural Templates**:
- **Organic Seeding Standard**: Hook → Pain Point Empathy → Solution & Mechanism → Real-World Proof → Soft/Medium CTA
- **Performance Ad Standard**: Hook → Core USP Delivery → Live Demo / Texture Test → Price & Bundle Anchor → Hard CTA
- **Comparison / Before-After Standard**: Hook → "Before" Struggle → Application Process → "After" Transformation → CTA

---

### 3. Pacing & Rhythm Design

**Pacing Elements**:
- Exact timestamps and duration of rapid-cut sequences
- How pacing shifts support the narrative (accelerate during bundle/feature lists; slow down on hero texture/effect shots)
- Synchronization between BGM beats and visual cuts
- Strategic pauses and breathing room so viewers can absorb key claims

**3 Core Pacing Models**:
- **Fast – Slow – Fast**: Grab attention fast (0–3s) → Slow down to demonstrate texture/results (3–20s) → Speed up for urgency and CTA (20–30s)
- **Progressive Acceleration**: Start with a calm relatable story and build momentum toward an urgent climax CTA
- **Pulse Rhythm**: Deliver a mini visual or verbal hook every 5–8 seconds (ideal for videos >45 seconds)

---

### 4. Visual Elements

**Visual Styling**:
- Color grading and filter mood (Warm cozy / Clean clinical / High-saturation vibrant / Natural daylight)
- Subtitle timing, font style, highlight colors, and safe-zone placement
- Stickers, arrows, callout boxes, and sound-effect badges
- Product presentation method (Handheld / Flat-lay / In-use on skin/body / Side-by-side comparison)

**Composition**:
- Subject placement (Center-framed / Rule of thirds / Diagonal dynamic)
- Background setting (Clean solid studio / Authentic bedroom or bathroom / Professional clinic or kitchen)
- Depth of field (Shallow depth of field for macro product textures)

---

### 5. Conversion Design

**CTA (Call to Action) Analysis**:
- CTA timing (End only / Mid-roll + End / Persistent visual badge)
- CTA mechanism (Spoken command / Pointing gesture to yellow basket / Comment prompt / Livestream redirect)
- Price anchoring strategy (Original price vs. deal price / Daily cost breakdown like *"chưa tới 5 nghìn một ngày"* / Cross-category comparison like *"rẻ hơn một ly trà sữa"*)
- Urgency & scarcity triggers (Limited time / Limited stock / Exclusive bonus gifts)

**Conversion Paths**:
- **Organic Seeding ("Video Review / Seeding")**: Video → Save/Follow/Comment → Search or tap basket to buy
- **Performance Ad ("TikTok Ads / Reels Ads / Chuyển đổi")**: Video → Tap Yellow Basket / Ad Card → Product Page or Livestream checkout

---

### 6. Ad Compliance Check (Vietnamese E-Commerce & TikTok/Reels Rules)

**Hard Red Lines**:
- **Prohibited Absolute Claims**: *"tốt nhất"*, *"số 1"*, *"cam kết 100%"*, *"tuyệt đối"*, *"duy nhất"*, *"đỉnh nhất"*, *"rẻ nhất"*
- **Unverified Miracle/Medical Promises**: *"trị dứt điểm"*, *"chữa khỏi hoàn toàn"*, *"tận gốc"*, *"vĩnh viễn"*, *"không tái phát"*
- **Timeframe + Guaranteed Biological Efficacy**: *"Trắng bật tông sau 3 ngày"*, *"Hết sạch mụn sau 24 giờ"*, *"Giảm 5kg trong 1 tuần"*
- **Deceptive Comparisons**: Disparaging named competitors or claiming a cosmetic/supplement replaces hospital treatment

**Platform & Category Nuances**:
- Paid ad creatives (TikTok Ads / Meta Reels Ads) undergo stricter automated OCR + ASR review than organic posts
- High-scrutiny categories: Cosmetics, dietary supplements (Thực phẩm chức năng), oral care, mother & baby, and F&B

---

### 7. Replicable Elements Extraction

**Core Question**: Which viral mechanics from this video can be directly transplanted to the user's product?

**Extraction Checklist**:
- Replicable hook sentence patterns (swap in the new product/category)
- Replicable storyboard progression (universal across the same category)
- Replicable visual styling (lighting, camera angles, subtitle highlighting)
- Replicable BGM and cut-rhythm pattern
- Replicable price-anchoring and CTA phrasing
- Non-replicable elements to avoid copying blindly (celebrity clout, expensive custom sets, brand-specific IP)

## Organic Seeding ("Video Review / Seeding") vs. Performance Ads ("TikTok Ads / Reels Ads / Chuyển đổi")

| Dimension | Video Review / Seeding (Organic) | TikTok Ads / Reels Ads (Performance) |
|-----------|----------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **Primary Goal** | Build trust, spark desire, organic reach & saves | Direct ROAS, immediate click-through & checkout |
| **Typical Duration** | 30–60 seconds | 15–30 seconds |
| **Hook Requirement** | Relatable empathy, curiosity, or surprising tip | Hard-hitting 3s hook (data anchor, acute pain point, or massive bundle) |
| **Content Vibe** | Authentic personal sharing, real-life context | High information density, immediate USP & proof delivery |
| **Voice & Tone** | Like a close friend sharing a genuine discovery | Like a sharp, persuasive advisor (energetic, never robotic) |
| **CTA Style** | Soft-to-medium (*"Mình để ở giỏ hàng góc trái cho bạn nào cần nhé"*) | Hard & urgent (*"Bấm ngay vào giỏ hàng săn deal Mua 1 Tặng 6 trước khi hết suất!"*) |
| **Ad Review Strictness** | Moderate (community guidelines + Shop rules) | Strict (automated ad policy scan on audio, text, and visuals) |
| **Primary Metrics** | 3s Hook Rate, Completion Rate, Saves, Shares | CTR, CVR, CPA, ROAS, CPM |

## 5 Essential Elements for Visual / Video Prompts

Whenever writing Gemini analysis prompts, storyboard visual descriptions, or AI video generation prompts, always specify all 5 elements:

1. **Subject** (Who / Which specific product and texture)
2. **Action** (What physical movement or demonstration is happening)
3. **Environment** (Where / What lighting and background setting)
4. **Style** (Visual mood, color grading, aesthetic)
5. **Camera** (Shot size + camera angle + movement, e.g., *Macro close-up, 45-degree angle, slow push-in*)

If any of the 5 elements is omitted, the creator or AI model will guess-often missing the intended visual impact.
